Active recall: close the book and try to reproduce the idea from memory. It feels worse than re-reading and works far better — the struggle is the point.

Every flashcard in [Anki](anki.md) is just active recall with a timer attached. Pair it with @spaced-repetition and you've automated the two things that actually move retention.
