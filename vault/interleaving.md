Interleaving means shuffling problem types instead of doing twenty of the same in a row. Blocked practice feels smooth and produces shallow learning; mixed practice feels clumsy and produces durable learning.

It forces your brain to first *decide which method applies* — the skill you actually need on the exam. Stack it on top of @spaced-repetition.
