Spaced repetition is the single highest-leverage study habit I know. You review a fact right before you'd forget it, so each review pushes the next one further out. The schedule does the worrying; you just show up.

The mechanism it exploits is the [forgetting curve](forgetting-curve.md) — memory decays exponentially unless you reinforce it.

In practice the engine underneath it is [active recall](active-recall.md): the *act of retrieving* is what strengthens the trace, not re-reading.

Mix it with [interleaving](interleaving.md) and you get most of the evidence-based wins in one stack.
