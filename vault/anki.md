Anki is a spaced-repetition flashcard app. The magic isn't the software, it's that it removes every excuse: it tells you exactly which cards to review today and nothing more.

It only works if the cards demand @active-recall — a card you can answer by recognition teaches you nothing. Keep them atomic, one fact each.
