A note you never revisit is a diary entry, not a tool. The fix is to make notes *findable and re-encounterable* — which is exactly what doomscrolling your own vault is for.

Two notes I keep coming back to: @feynman-technique for compressing an idea, and @dual-coding for making it stick. The best note is one you'd actually want to scroll past again.
