We forget on a curve. Within an hour of learning something, more than half of it is already gone; within a day, most of it. The decay is roughly exponential.

But the curve is not destiny — each time you successfully recall, the next forgetting curve gets flatter. That flattening is the whole reason @spaced-repetition works.
